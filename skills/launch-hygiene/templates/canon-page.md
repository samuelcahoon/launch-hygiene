# Launch canon

The one place the approved launch messaging lives. Claude reads this page at the start of every chat and saves it back after every change. Edit the JSON below, or tell Claude what changed and it will update it for you.

**Version:** 1 · **Updated:** [date] · **Owner:** [you]

## How to use it

- A decision changes a line: the new line goes under `approved`, the old one under `retired`, then run a drift check.
- A number, customer name or security claim: add it under `claims` with its source, who approved it and when to review it.
- A new place the messaging lives: add it under `surfaces` with its owner.
